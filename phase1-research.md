**Clear vs frosted:** Clear glass shows the LED as points, so you need an inner diffuser such as a satin acrylic core rod or tube. A clear outer tube over a frosted inner one gives depth, the "cold drink" look, and is the strongest option for this aesthetic. Fully frosted glass gives an even glow but loses the sparkle.

## 3. Frosted acrylic as a primary material
**Cast vs extruded**
- *Cast:* better optical clarity, stays clean under the laser, less internal stress (less crazing near solvent glue), polishes well. Costs more and its thickness varies more.
- *Extruded:* tight thickness tolerance and cheaper. Bends cleanly on a strip heater. It is more prone to stress cracking with solvent cement, so anneal it after cutting.
- *Tubes:* TAP Plastics cast clear tube https://www.tapplastics.com/product/plastics/plastic_rods_tubes_shapes/clear_cast_acrylic_tubing/462 (by the foot: …/clear_cast_by_ft/653) · U.S. Plastic frosted extruded tube, 2 in, **$5.88/ft**: https://www.usplastic.com/catalog/item.aspx?catid=440&clickid=newitems&itemid=166769 · TAP frosted acrylic **rods** (solid light-guide cores): https://www.tapplastics.com/product/plastics/plastic_rods_tubes_shapes/frosted_acrylic_rods/149

**Satin grades**
- **ACRYLITE Satinice 0D010 DF** (called PLEXIGLAS Satinice outside the Americas; both are Röhm/Polyvantis): colorless, satin on both sides, with diffusing beads through the whole sheet that hide LED hotspots. Transmission is 91% at 1/16 in, 86% at 1/8 in, and 80% at 1/4 in. Sold as sheet and tube; the texture survives thermoforming. Datasheet: https://www.acrylite.co/files/content/acrylite.co/documents/product-information/ACRYLITE-Satinice-Technical-Information.pdf
- **Satinice WD85x "optimum light diffusion enhanced"**: no white pigment, so it keeps CRI high and avoids color shift (about 92% transmission for WD852 and 85% for WD853). Good for keeping a cool white crisp instead of grey. https://www.acrylite.co/products/brands/acrylite-satinice/optimum-light-diffusion-enhanced
- **Buy:** Canal Plastics 0D010 DF cut sheet **from $5.69**: https://www.canalplastic.com/products/0d010-df-frosted-satin-ice-acrylic-sheet · TAP Plastics cut-to-size: https://www.tapplastics.com/product/plastics/cut_to_size_plastic/satinice_clear/656 · Polyvantis shop (EU) **from €138/m²** for 6 mm: https://www.polyvantis-shop.com/gb/plexiglas-satinice/

**Tube vs sheet:** A tube gives the column form with no seam, but you're limited to stock diameters. Sheet can be laser-cut into ribs or slabs, or bent into a U or cylinder on a strip heater. A rolled cylinder leaves a visible seam, which you can hide toward the back.

**Fabrication:** CO₂ laser cuts acrylic cleanly. Cast leaves a flame-polished edge; extruded leaves a slightly rougher one. Line-bend at roughly 150–160 °C (general PMMA practice, not taken from these sources). Join with solvent cement such as Weld-On 4 or with mechanical clamps. Keep LEDs physically away from the plastic: PMMA is combustible according to the Acrylite datasheet, and the COB strips here draw about 15 W/m, so mount them on an aluminum channel.

**Edge-lit:** Clear cast sheet edge-lit from a slot in the base gives a glowing outline plus whatever you engrave (a laser-etched "ion" logo or a wave pattern lights up). Satinice edge-lit glows evenly across the whole face. For edge lighting, use 6–10 mm thickness and polish the entry edge.

**Acrylic vs glass**
| | Borosilicate / blown glass | Satin acrylic |
|---|---|---|
| Look | Real sparkle and weight, most premium | Velvety, even glow; satin hides fingerprints. Looks less "real" when clear. |
| Cost | $15–25 per tube, plus custom blowing if you go that way | $6/ft tube, about $6–20 per cut sheet |
| Buildability | Hard to cut or drill (diamond tools), fragile | Laser, bend, glue, tap. About 20× the impact strength of glass and half the weight (datasheet). |
| Heat | Fine | Needs a heat path and distance from the LEDs |

**Recommendation:** Prototype everything in Satinice. For the final build, use either an all-Satinice body, or a clear borosilicate outer tube over a Satinice inner diffuser (the hero version).

**COB vs strip:** COB gives a continuous line with no dots behind frosted material. SMD addressable strips give you animation (a slow "fizz" shimmer) but show dots unless the diffuser is 20 mm or more away.

**Fresh, not cheap-blue:**
1. Build the base light from **high-CRI white at 5000–6500 K**, never from the blue LED channel alone.
2. Add only a *touch* of blue from RGBW, or tint it with the diffuser (pale-blue acrylic or glass).
3. Use pigment-free diffusers (Satinice WD85x) so the white doesn't turn grey.
4. Dim warm-ward at night (tunable white) so the lamp doesn't feel clinical.
5. Avoid RGB-only strips: their "white" is bluish with low CRI, which is what makes cheap lamps look cheap.

## 5. Controller and power
- **MCU:** Seeed XIAO ESP32-C3 **$4.99** (https://www.seeedstudio.com/Seeed-XIAO-ESP32C3-p-5431.html); Adafruit QT Py ESP32-S3 $12.50 (https://www.adafruit.com/product/5426) or QT Py ESP32-C3 (https://www.adafruit.com/product/5405).
- **All-in-one WLED + USB-C PD:** **MagWLED-1**, ESP32-C3, 5 or 12 V PD up to 36 W, MOSFET cut-off, **$29.90**: https://magwled.com/products/dgrz-led-cpd-36-1-mini · LocalBytes, ESP32-C3, PD 12 V, £14.95: https://www.mylocalbytes.com/products/wled-strip-controller · XIAO LED driver board, 5 V/3 A or 12 V/2 A, £5.70: https://thepihut.com/products/led-driver-board-for-seeed-studio-xiao
- **PD trigger (custom build):** INGZ CYPD3177, rotary-selectable 5–20 V, $38: https://www.tindie.com/products/ingbz/usb-c-pd-trigger-board-with-screw-terminal/ · Generic fixed-12 V boards cost a few dollars (example: https://punoscho.in/product/usb-qc-pd-afc-trigger-decoy-board-module-12v/).
- **Dimming / flicker:** On ESP32, WLED's analog (PWM) output defaults to **~19.5 kHz**, and 0.15.0 added a selectable PWM frequency plus 12-bit resolution. Keep it at 19.5 kHz or higher. That is far above the visible range and camera-safe, and 12-bit avoids stepping at low brightness. https://kno.wled.ge/features/settings/ · https://github.com/Aircoookie/WLED/releases/tag/v0.15.0 · CCT handling: https://kno.wled.ge/features/cct/
- **Tunable-white COB wiring:** two logic-level N-MOSFETs (warm and cool channels) driven from ESP32 PWM. Addressable SK6812 strips need only one data pin (MagWLED or the XIAO driver handles the level shifting).
- **Controls:** capacitive touch (a pad hidden behind the acrylic or glass, using the ESP32-S3's native touch or a TTP223 module), a rotary encoder (EC11) for a knob, and WLED buttons or usermods. Home Assistant picks up WLED automatically on the local network.

## 6. Recommended starter path
**Form:** A, the glass column, roughly 50–70 mm across and 250–350 mm tall. It has a satin acrylic inner diffuser, an optional clear borosilicate outer tube, and a machined or printed base that holds the electronics.
**Light:** tunable-white high-CRI COB (Circ A5000 to start, Yuji CRI-MAX for the final), plus a short 6000 K RGBW addressable accent for the "fizz".
**Brain:** XIAO ESP32-C3 with WLED, powered over USB-C PD at 12 V.
