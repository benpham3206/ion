# ion: metal finish study

These are recolors of the three existing concept renders in `docs/concepts/`, not new renders. Only the metal (the two plates and the base) was changed. Glass, glow, background and lighting are as rendered. The shading comes from the original image's luminance, remapped to each finish. The copper concept was converted to grey as well.

| Finish | Target | Surface |
|---|---|---|
| Gunmetal grey | `#3E4247` | satin |
| Metallic ash grey | `#B9BEC4` | fine brushed |

`ion-finish-board.png` compares all three concepts side by side: original, gunmetal, ash.

## Take per concept
- **Clear glass, warm core:** gunmetal. The dark plates frame the warm glass and let the glow lead. Ash is close to the original aluminum.
- **Cool clear:** gunmetal. It gives crisp contrast on white and makes the blue panel brighter. Ash is a slightly darker, cooler take on the original.
- **Warm amber (from copper):** ash. It catches the amber light and keeps the lamp legible in the dark room. Gunmetal nearly disappears, although it is moodier.
